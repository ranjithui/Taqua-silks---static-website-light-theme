## Read this before you launch

Three caveats, in order of how much they matter:

1. **The fabric does not match several card labels.** This pool is almost entirely
   **printed cotton batik**. It is *not* Kanchipuram, *not* Banarasi, *not* bridal silk.
   The cards below marked ⚠️ therefore show a fabric that contradicts their title.
   That is fine as a placeholder, but it misrepresents the product to a customer —
   **replace these before you take orders.**

2. **No model release.** PDM waives the photographer's copyright. It does *not* grant
   permission to use an identifiable person's likeness in advertising. Every image here
   shows a recognisable model. Low risk given they were published as shop catalog
   photos, but it is not zero.

3. **PDM here is self-asserted** by the uploader, not verified by Flickr or Openverse.

## ⚠️ These are low resolution — replace when you can

Each crop is only **265 × 240 px** in the source mockup. I upscaled 2× (Lanczos) to
530 × 480, but **upscaling cannot add detail that was never captured** — on a phone or
any high-DPI screen these will look noticeably soft compared to the adult cards.

They are also **landscape** (1.10:1) while every card is **4:5 portrait**, so CSS
`object-fit: cover` trims the left and right edges. The figures are centred, so this
mostly works, but there is some loss.

**The fix:** regenerate the 8 images **individually** at full size in whatever tool
produced the mockup (ask for vertical 4:5, ~1024 px wide or larger), then drop them in
over these files using the same names. Everything else is already wired.
