# Kids Collection — photo shoot guide

**6 photos needed.** Shoot them, name them, drop them in this folder. They appear on the
site automatically — no code changes, no need to tell me.

A modern phone camera is entirely good enough. The background and lighting matter far
more than the camera.

## Use a child display mannequin, not a child

Strongly recommended, for two reasons:

- **No consent problem.** Photographs of a real, identifiable child used in advertising
  need written permission from a parent or guardian. A mannequin removes this entirely.
- **It matches the rest of the site.** Your adult cards are garment-focused. Mannequins
  keep the whole page looking like one catalog.

If you do photograph a real child, get **written parental consent** covering commercial
and online use, and keep it on file.

## Setup

**Background** — plain and pale. A white or warm-peach bedsheet taped smooth to a wall
works perfectly. No furniture, doorways, shop shelves or people in frame.

**Light** — shoot near a large window during the day, with the garment facing the window.
Turn the room lights off; mixing daylight with tube light causes colour casts. Overcast
days are ideal. Avoid direct sun, which blows out zari detail.

**Camera** — hold the phone at the garment's **mid-height**, not looking down at it.
Step back and zoom slightly rather than shooting up close; this stops the skirt flaring
out of proportion.

**Framing** — vertical, with a little clear space above and below the garment. Leave room
around the edges; the cards crop slightly.

**Consistency matters more than perfection.** Same background, same distance, same light
for all six. Shoot them in one session. Six near-identical setups look far more premium
than six individually nicer but mismatched photos.

## Before saving

1. **Crop to vertical 4:5** (e.g. 900 × 1125 px). Every card on the site is 4:5, so
   cropping yourself means the site won't crop somewhere awkward.
2. **Resize to roughly 900–1200 px wide.** Straight-from-phone files are 4–8 MB and will
   make the page slow. Aim for **under 400 KB** each.
3. Save as `.jpg`, quality ~80.

Free tools: [squoosh.app](https://squoosh.app) (browser, no install), Windows Photos, or
any phone editing app.

## When you're done

Copy the six files into this folder (`images/photos/`) and refresh the site. That's it.

If a card still shows the gold-motif placeholder, the filename is wrong — check for typos
like `drape15` or `drape-15 (1).jpg`.
