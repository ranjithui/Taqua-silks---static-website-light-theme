A Legacy of Craft

Backed by ESSA Garments

Taqua Silks is proudly backed by ESSA Garments Private Limited, one of Tiruppur's leading integrated textile and apparel manufacturers. Founded in 1994 by Mr. S. Syed Sadiq Ali, ESSA Garments began as a knitwear manufacturing enterprise with a vision to deliver world-class quality while making fashionable clothing accessible to every family. Through decades of innovation and dedication, the company has grown into a vertically integrated textile organization serving customers across India and international markets.

Building on this strong foundation, the TAQUA brand was introduced in 2006 to strengthen the company's retail presence and bring premium-quality apparel directly to consumers. Today, Taqua Silks carries forward this legacy by offering an extensive collection of bridal silk sarees, traditional wear, designer ethnic collections, contemporary fashion, men's apparel, kids' wear, and everyday essentials — all under one roof.

Supported by ESSA Garments' advanced manufacturing ecosystem, every product reflects the company's commitment to superior craftsmanship, stringent quality standards, and continuous innovation. With modern production facilities capable of manufacturing over 100,000 garments per day, an extensive nationwide distribution network, and ISO 9001-certified quality processes, ESSA has established itself as a trusted name in the Indian textile industry.
