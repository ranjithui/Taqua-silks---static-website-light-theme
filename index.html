Our Heritage

Preserving India's Silk Legacy

Taqua Silks was born from a single conviction — that the timeless art of Indian handloom deserves a stage as regal as the weaves themselves. For over two decades, we have worked hand-in-hand with master artisans across Kanchipuram, Varanasi, and Mysore.

Every saree that carries our name is a quiet rebellion against the ordinary — pure silk, real zari, and motifs passed down through generations of weaving families.

  • Direct-from-weaver sourcing
  • Authentic Silk Mark certified
  • Fair, ethical craftsmanship
  • Heirloom-grade quality
