Our Heritage

A Family Store Trusted on Municipal Office Road

Taqua Silks has been a trusted name on Municipal Office Road, Tiruppur, for over 25 years — a family-run silk saree and clothing store where quality is never left to chance. Every saree carries authentic Silk Mark certification and real zari, checked by hand before it reaches you.

Three generations of families across Tiruppur, Coimbatore, and Kerala keep coming back to us — from a bride's first Kanchipuram silk saree to her son's dhoti for the same wedding. That kind of loyalty isn't built overnight; it's earned one honest purchase at a time.
